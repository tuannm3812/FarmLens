Meet FarmLens 🌱 — a friendly starting point for understanding what could grow on your land.

I’ve been building a local prototype for NSW farmers and landowners, bringing climate, Australian soil data and crop requirements into one map-led experience.

Drop a pin and explore:

• A land snapshot with modelled soil, weather and dated NSW imagery  
• Three crop candidates, with transparent factor scores and limitations  
• Separate water and gross-margin scenarios with editable assumptions  
• Finn, a small report companion who explains the information already on screen

Built with Python, a custom web interface and Codex. The most interesting challenge has been making uncertainty visible without overwhelming the page.

This is a working local showcase, not a validated agronomy service. The scores are screening indices; they don’t predict yield or profit. Finn uses local rules, with optional model-generated crop explanations available separately.

I’d love feedback from farmers, agronomists and product builders: what would make this a useful first conversation about a piece of land?

Code, setup and project notes: https://github.com/tuannm3812/FarmLens

Indicative only. Check with a local agronomist.
