01 / Evidence & method

Make the reasoning inspectable.

ERA5 climate + modelled CSIRO/TERN SLGA soil + approximate FAO EcoCrop profiles. Seven weighted factors and source-linked NSW checks expose constraints.

Water pilot: daily rain and FAO-56 ET0 × crop coefficients, with an editable storage balance.

02 / Engineering

Designed to show uncertainty.

Python engine + custom web UI. Missing soil pH withholds the shortlist; absent factors stay unassessed. Cached evidence retains source status.

230 Python + 11 JavaScript tests in the recorded pre-showcase run. Software checks are not agronomic validation.

03 / Limits & next steps

From prototype to local validation.

Modelled inputs and illustrative water defaults are not farm measurements. No yield or profit forecasts. Scores and water estimates are uncalibrated.

Next: agronomist review, measured soil-water comparisons and locally calibrated crop timing.
