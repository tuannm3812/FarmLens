# FarmLens showcase brief

As agreed on 20 October 2026, finish a polished local showcase for a LinkedIn
project post. The identity should feel like a friendly farming companion.

The visitor chooses a NSW point, understands its modelled land/climate context,
compares up to three crop candidates and sees the main limitations. Optional
tools explore historical water needs and user-entered budget scenarios.

Completion means a coherent Home / Explore / How it works experience, reusable
friendly branding, concise report content, actual dated land imagery, a bounded
Ask FarmLens guide, verified fallback states and updated presentation materials.
Keep the existing transparent scoring engine and disclose its validation limits.

Public deployment, voice, accounts, live market predictions and additional
crop-model calibration are outside today's scope. Prepare share materials;
the user publishes their own LinkedIn post.
