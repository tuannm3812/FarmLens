## Decisions and review focus
- Ten full calendar years relative to Australia/Sydney today; 2016–2025 for the checkpoint date.
- Mean annual temperature = annual mean of daily `(max + min)/2`, then mean across years.
- Coldest month = lowest climatological monthly mean temperature; frost proxy = mean daily minimum in that month. It is not a frost probability.
- Driest three months = sum of the three lowest climatological monthly rainfall totals (not necessarily consecutive).
- Soil depth weights = 5/30, 10/30 and 15/30; divide phh2o, clay, sand and soc by 10, yielding pH, %, %, g/kg respectively.
- Reject missing/null/incomplete data rather than silently reducing rainfall or inventing zero soil values.
- Cache keys include source and coordinates rounded to two decimal places; historical namespace includes year range. Validate cached payloads too.
- Only successful real API payloads enter the cache. Offline regional estimates are clearly labelled and never persisted as observations.
- Expired forecast cache must retain its dates and be labelled stale; regional fallback is not a forecast.
- Tests block all real network requests; exercise timeouts, malformed responses, invalid cache and unwritable cache.
