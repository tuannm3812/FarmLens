Every score has a reason.

FarmLens combines historical climate, Australian modelled soil and approximate FAO crop profiles. It is a first screening tool; it does not predict yield or profitability.

Source-linked NSW guidance checks sit alongside the broad scores. Known conflicts withhold a candidate; missing evidence stays unassessed. All remaining candidates still need local checks, and meeting one check is not field validation. Reported drainage is your observation and is never inferred from soil texture.

Two soil factors

Texture · 15%. A broad proxy groups soil as heavy (clay ≥35%), light (otherwise sand ≥65%), or medium. The crop's EcoCrop preferences score 100; listed tolerances score 70; unlisted classes score 40. These classes and penalties are demo assumptions, not a full texture classification or absolute crop limits.

Carbon condition · 5%. The shared condition score increases through 0%, 0.8%, 1.5% and 2% organic carbon (50, 70, 90 and 100 points), using the modelled 0–30 cm layer. This uncalibrated curve is not crop-specific fertility or a carbon target.

Missing factors are unassessed and their weights excluded. Missing soil pH withholds recommendations. Any zero factor caps the total at 20. Drainage, salinity, management, cultivar and market access still need local assessment.
