01 / THE METHOD

How FarmLens works

  1. Select a NSW point.

    Data is requested for your selection. Modelled grid cells provide context; they are not a field survey.

  2. Screen crop requirements.

    Temperature, rainfall, pH and frost are compared with approximate crop profiles. Texture and organic carbon add experimental screening factors; configured fire evidence can also contribute.

  3. Show the reasons.

    Factors are combined using the weights shown in the app. A zero factor caps the total at 20/100. Missing factors remain unassessed; missing soil pH withholds a shortlist.

  4. Check local constraints.

    Source-linked NSW checks can withhold conflicting candidates. Drainage is your observation. A high score is not a probability of success or field validation.

Exact weights, factor scores and local checks are available in the explorer’s Scoring details and crop profiles.

02 / PLANNING TOOLS

Explore a scenario.
Keep the assumptions visible.

Water feasibility

Compare seasonal demand and irrigation gaps across historical seasons for wheat, barley and canola. Soil storage and efficiency are editable assumptions. This is not an irrigation schedule.

Crop budgets

Enter yield, price and costs to calculate gross margin and break-even points. Optional NSW examples are archived 2012 budgets. Gross margin excludes overheads, finance and tax; it is not net profit.

Vegetation cover

Dated satellite samples show green vegetation, dry vegetation and bare ground. Cloud or missing evidence leaves gaps. Pixel cover does not establish soil health or crop yield.

03 / DATA & SOURCES

Trace the evidence.

Source dates and cache status are shown in each land report.

Open-Meteo ↗

Ten complete years of historical reanalysis, plus a separate seven-day forecast. History informs crop scores; the forecast adds weekly context.

Soil and Landscape Grid of Australia ↗

Australian modelled soil properties, approximately 90 m resolution, averaged across 0–30 cm. Local soil sampling can change the interpretation.

Digital Earth Australia ↗

Dated land-cover classification and quality-filtered fractional cover at 30 m. These provide context and do not affect crop scores.

FAO EcoCrop ↗ + NSW guidance

Approximate global crop ranges alongside source-linked NSW constraints. Texture penalties and the carbon curve remain uncalibrated demo assumptions.

NSW enterprise budgets ↗

Gross-margin methodology and explicit archived examples. Current yield, price and costs must come from your scenario.

NASA FIRMS ↗

Optional historical active-fire detections when configured. Detections do not predict future fires.

04 / USING THE PROTOTYPE

Know what is shared.

Address searches and pins rounded to two decimals go to OpenStreetMap Nominatim. Use public places. The selected point is sent to the relevant environmental data services.

Ask FarmLens uses local rules to answer supported questions about your report. Typed questions stay in the browser and clear when the report changes. Finn is a software guide, not an agronomist.

If OpenAI explanations are configured, only the crop numbers are sent—not the address or coordinates. Otherwise, explanations use local rules.

Land imagery loads directly from NSW Spatial Services. Survey-block dates are shown where available; the mosaic is not live imagery or a property boundary. Imagery and attribution ↗

The last assessed coordinates and growing scenario stay in this browser tab so you can return from these pages. Search text, full address labels and budget inputs are not saved by this navigation feature.

Failed services use valid cached data where possible. Climate may fall back to labelled regional demo estimates; unavailable Australian soil and satellite evidence remain unassessed.
