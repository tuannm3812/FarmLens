# Crop data dictionary and provenance

Version: demo-2026-10-20. `crops.csv` contains the 15 requested profiles. The temperature (°C), annual rainfall (mm) and pH ranges were transcribed from the linked FAO EcoCrop data sheets on 20 October 2026. These are broad global species requirements, **approximate for a NSW screening application**, not locally validated response curves. Each row carries its exact source URL and EcoCrop ID. No crop threshold is inferred from an LLM prediction.

For each prefix `temp`, `rain`, `ph`, the order is `abs_min < opt_min <= opt_max < abs_max`. The score is zero at/outside either absolute limit, 100 within the optimal interval, and linear between. Numeric ranges are source values; the linear interpolation is FarmLens's requested scoring method.

## Explicit assumptions (not FAO measurements)

- `texture_optimal` and `texture_tolerated` are semicolon-separated class lists transcribed from each linked EcoCrop sheet on 20 October 2026. The extraction record is `texture-source-records.json`. `wide` covers all three mineral proxy groups; `organic` is retained as source data but is not inferred from a carbon percentage. FarmLens groups SLGA fractions as heavy when clay ≥35%, otherwise light when sand ≥65%, otherwise medium. This is an uncalibrated broad grouping, not a USDA classification: SLGA's sand definition (20–2000 μm) differs from USDA sand. The score is 100 for a preferred group, 70 for a tolerated group, 40 for an unlisted group. These numbers are assumptions; an unlisted group is not an absolute crop-growth limit. Invalid or inconsistent clay/sand fractions are unassessed.
- Organic carbon contributes a small **shared soil-condition proxy**, not a crop-specific nutrient score. Convert SLGA g/kg to percent by dividing by 10. Interpolate (SOC %, score): (0,50), (0.8,70), (1.5,90), (2,100), remaining at 100 above 2%. This curve is a **FarmLens assumption**, applied to the modelled 0–30 cm average. It is informed by the direction of soil-carbon effects in [FAO GAEZ](https://gaez-v4-data.fao.org/data/documentation/GAEZ%20v4%20Model%20Documentation.pdf), pp90–92, and the general reference in [NSW soil-test interpretation](https://www.dpi.nsw.gov.au/about-us/services/laboratory-services/soil-health/interpret). It is not a published all-crop response curve, fertiliser recommendation or carbon target. The shared term can change totals without distinguishing crops at one site. [Soil CRC](https://soilcrc.com.au/resources/amendments-to-improve-the-productivity-of-sandy-soils-fact-sheet/) demonstrates context-dependent sandy-soil amendment responses; its yield-response thresholds are not treated as crop suitability limits here. Missing/invalid carbon stays unassessed. Carbon alone cannot cause a zero cap.
- Weights in scoring v2: temperature 20%, rainfall 24%, pH 16%, frost 12%, fire 8%, texture 15%, carbon 5%. They remain uncalibrated. Missing-factor weights are excluded and the remainder renormalized. The prior five weights were all scaled by 0.8, preserving their relative influence.

- `frost_tolerance`: high/medium/low **working demo classes**, informed by species descriptions but not calibrated against crop-stage damage. The model maps the coldest monthly mean minimum to a one-sided score: high goes from 0 at −8°C to 100 at 0°C; medium −2°C to 4°C; low 0°C to 8°C. Above the upper threshold remains 100. These cutoffs are FarmLens assumptions, **not EcoCrop killing temperatures**. Frost occurrence and reproductive-stage frost damage need a later model.
- `drought_tolerance`: qualitative working classes, not used in the numeric total. Barley's EcoCrop sheet explicitly records drought tolerance; other entries remain unvalidated working classifications.
- `fire_sensitivity`: all profiles currently use conservative `high`; there is insufficient evidence here to claim comparative fire resilience. Exposure low/medium/high maps to 0/1/2, and this high-sensitivity placeholder scores 100/50/0. The configurable implementation supports other sensitivity classes, but the dataset does not assert them. Missing fire exposure produces an unassessed score and no fire weight. This is a demo risk flag, not a model of wildfire damage, insurance risk or smoke taint.
- `growing_months`: rough evaluation horizons (five or six months for annuals), not sourced cultivar maturity values or forecasts. Twelve for perennials/pasture is a storage default; those crops are excluded from seasonal mode.
- `planting_months`: deliberately limited month-level **screening windows**, simplified by FarmLens from the local references below. They do not cover all regions, cultivars, early/late sowing or grazing uses. A month inside this list means “consider discussing this crop,” not “safe to sow now.” Coastal and far-west application is especially provisional.
- `requires_irrigation`: rice-only NSW demo eligibility constraint. Rainfed rice exists globally. Irrigation availability does not increase any score without a quantified water budget; natural annual rainfall is still displayed and scored.
- Citrus represents sweet orange; improved pasture represents perennial ryegrass, not all species or mixtures. Canola uses the broad Brassica napus profile.

## NSW map boundary

`nsw_boundary.geojson` is an extract of the Australian Bureau of Statistics [ASGS 2021 state layer](https://geo.abs.gov.au/arcgis/rest/services/ASGS2021/STE/MapServer/0), licensed CC BY 4.0. Downloaded 20 October 2026 with `state_code_2021='1'`, `outSR=4326`, `maxAllowableOffset=0.001`, `geometryPrecision=5`. It retains the ACT exclusion and NSW islands. The simplified geometry is for demo screening, not cadastral or legal decisions. The 2021 edition is a fixed reference version, not a claim to be the latest ABS release.

## NSW guidance overlay (v3)

`nsw_guidance.json` records separately reviewed NSW requirements and unresolved site checks for all 15 profiles. It does **not** overwrite EcoCrop ranges or calibrate the seven weights. Rainfed macadamia below 1,200 mm/year and lentil at or below 300 mm/year are withheld when fresh compatible historical rainfall is available. Five drainage-sensitive crops are withheld when the user reports poor drainage; unknowns remain unassessed. Source-backed conflicts remain inspectable in `guidance_excluded` with the original scores. User-reported drainage is reset when the point changes.

CaCl2 pH recommendations are never compared directly with SLGA pH in water. A passed rainfall/drainage check is not a validated crop. See the [NSW review](../docs/research/2026-10-20-nsw-scoring-validation.md) for source versions, compatibility decisions and the empirical validation still needed.
